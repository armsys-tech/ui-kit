About this ranking

How we collect, verify and rank public API activity.

How is request volume measured?

Volumes are aggregated from verified gateway telemetry of services that opted into public listing. Bot and health-check traffic is excluded.

How often is the ranking updated?

Rankings refresh every hour. Historical snapshots are kept for 24 months.

Can I list my own API?

Yes. Enable public listing in your project settings; your service appears after 7 days of verified traffic.

Why are some rows hidden?

Positions beyond the top 10 are available to signed-in users to prevent automated scraping.
