FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Contact our team.

What counts as an event?

Each message published to a stream counts as one event, regardless of how many subscribers receive it. Heartbeats and acknowledgements are free.

What happens if I exceed my plan?

Nothing breaks. Overage is billed per million events and you'll receive alerts at 80% and 100% of your included volume.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits and education?

Yes — qualifying organisations receive 50% off Starter and Growth. Contact sales with your details.

Which payment methods are supported?

All major cards, SEPA direct debit and invoicing on yearly plans.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us about your workload and we'll recommend a plan — usually within one business day.
